Frequently Asked Questions

Consultations & Bookings

What details do I need to provide for an accurate Kundli reading?

To prepare your accurate natal chart (Lagna Kundli), we need three key details: your exact Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Place of Birth. If your exact birth time is unknown, we can perform Birth Time Rectification (BTR) prior to the reading.

How are the online consultation sessions conducted?

Online sessions are conducted securely via Google Meet or Zoom. Once you book an appointment, you will receive an automated email containing the calendar invite, session link, and instructions on how to prepare your questions.

Is my personal information and consultation confidential?

100% yes. We adhere to strict privacy protocols. Your birth details, personal history, and discussion points are never shared with any third party.

Remedies & Astrological Guidance

Do you sell gemstones directly or provide lab certificates?

We provide genuine, unheated, and government-lab certified Vedic gemstones upon request. Every gemstone comes with an authenticity certificate detailing its carat weight, origin, and optical properties.

Can remedies alter planetary influences completely?

Planetary transits represent karmic patterns. Vedic remedies (Mantra, Daan, Yantra, Gemstones) act as a cosmic umbrella—they mitigate harsh planetary effects and enhance favorable outcomes, allowing you to navigate difficult phases with clarity and protection.
